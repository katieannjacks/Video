Meta-questions — the patterns the 17 don't quite catch

  • Am I going deep in one niche/vertical where I'm becoming known — or spreading thin across unrelated verticals so reputation and referrals never compound?
    Niching vs. spreading.
  • What share of my hours this month went to work for paying clients vs. building things on spec for hypothetical future customers?
    Building for paying clients vs. on spec.
  • Could a stranger repeat my offer back to me in one sentence — who it's for, what painful problem it solves, and what it costs — or is my offer still fuzzy and custom every time?
    Productized offer clarity.

These aren't scored — they're for the journal. Jot your honest answer each month before you save.

How to use this scorecard

Once a month, rate each of the 17 questions from 1 (drifting toward failure) to 5 (doing the right thing). Answer fast and honestly — your gut already knows.

  • The header shows your live total /85, average, current weakest area, and the change versus your last saved month.
  • Hit Save this month's snapshot to lock in the month. Snapshots are stored locally in this browser — nothing leaves your machine.
  • The chart plots your total over time so you can see drift before it becomes a crisis.
  • Reflect on weakest areas builds a prompt from your lowest scores to paste back to Claude for a 30-day improvement plan specific to your agency.

The goal isn't a perfect score — it's catching drift early. An 85 every month probably means you're being too easy on yourself. Look for the dips and the questions you keep avoiding.
