# Built by Beards — Website Brief

A ready-to-use packet for building the **Built by Beards** website (Clayton, NC).
Because Facebook and Lovable both block automated scraping, this brief carries
the brand info **in text** so Lovable can build from it — and gives you a short
checklist of the few things only you can grab from the Facebook page.

---

## Step 1 — Paste this prompt into Lovable

> Build a modern, mobile-friendly one-page marketing website for a local
> contractor called **Built by Beards**, based in **Clayton, North Carolina**
> (serving the Clayton / Four Oaks / Johnston County area).
>
> **Vibe:** rugged but professional, trustworthy, hand-crafted. Think warm
> wood tones, charcoal/black, a clean sans-serif for headings, and a beard /
> craftsmanship motif. Friendly small-business feel, not corporate.
>
> **Sections (top to bottom):**
> 1. **Sticky header** — logo/wordmark "Built by Beards", nav links (Services,
>    Gallery, About, Reviews, Contact), and a prominent "Get a Free Estimate"
>    button that scrolls to the contact form.
> 2. **Hero** — full-width background photo of a finished project, headline
>    like *"Quality Craftsmanship, Built by Beards"*, a short subhead
>    (*"Carpentry, remodeling & general contracting in Clayton, NC"*), and two
>    buttons: "Get a Free Estimate" and "See Our Work".
> 3. **Services** — a grid of cards, one per service, each with an icon and a
>    one-line description:
>    - Kitchen Remodeling
>    - Bathroom Remodeling
>    - Custom Carpentry & Trim
>    - Tile Installation
>    - Screen Rooms (installation & repair)
>    - General Contracting
> 4. **Gallery** — a responsive photo grid / lightbox for before-and-after and
>    finished-project shots (placeholder images for now; I'll swap in real
>    photos).
> 5. **About** — short story about the business: a local, family-run contractor
>    known for craftsmanship and honest work. Leave a clearly marked spot for
>    the owner's name and the "our story" paragraph.
> 6. **Reviews / Testimonials** — 2–3 testimonial cards with star ratings and
>    a "Read more on Facebook" link.
> 7. **Contact** — a simple estimate-request form (Name, Phone, Email, Project
>    Type dropdown matching the services above, Message) plus clickable phone
>    number, service-area note ("Clayton, NC & surrounding Johnston County"),
>    and a link to the Facebook page.
> 8. **Footer** — business name, "Licensed & Insured" line, service area,
>    Facebook link, and copyright.
>
> **Tech:** responsive, fast, accessible. Use placeholder images where I
> haven't supplied photos. Make all the editable copy (phone, owner name,
> testimonials, address) easy to find and change.
